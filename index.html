How it works

  • The bridge is modeled as an ideal 2D truss: pin joints, straight two-force members, weight of the members neglected.
  • Supports: pin at L0 (left) and roller at L6 (right). 12 joints, 21 members, 3 reactions: 2·12 = 21 + 3, so it is statically determinate.
  • Forces are found with the method of joints: at every joint ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0, solved all at once.
  • Loads can act on the deck joints (L) or the top joints (U). Weights use g = 9.81 m/s² (500 g → 4.91 N).
  • A moving weight between two joints is shared by those joints in proportion to how close it is (like a deck plank resting on two floor beams). At 25 % of the way from L2 to L3, L2 carries 75 % and L3 carries 25 %.
  • The graph is the member's influence line: its force for every position of the weight. Look for members that switch between T and C as the weight passes!
